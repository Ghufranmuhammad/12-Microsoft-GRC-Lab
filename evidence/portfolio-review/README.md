Evidence Folder

FICTIONAL / EDUCATIONAL PORTFOLIO REVIEW

This evidence folder is part of a fictional and educational GRC portfolio project created to demonstrate practical understanding of information security governance, risk management, compliance, audit readiness, and ISO 27001 concepts.

All screenshots, examples, documents, spreadsheets, and other materials in this folder are provided for learning and portfolio demonstration purposes only.

Important Disclaimer

- DesertTech Solutions is a fictional organization created for this portfolio.
- The examples and screenshots are educational simulations and do not represent evidence from a real company.
- No confidential, proprietary, personal, classified, or restricted company information is included.
- Any sample evidence has been created, anonymized, or simulated specifically for educational purposes.
- The materials should not be interpreted as an official ISO 27001 audit, certification assessment, regulatory assessment, or professional consulting engagement.
- The evidence demonstrates how a GRC professional may organize and review supporting documentation in a controlled assessment environment.

Purpose of This Folder

The purpose of this folder is to demonstrate how supporting evidence can be:

- Collected and organized
- Mapped to security controls
- Reviewed for completeness
- Used to support audit findings
- Linked to risk and compliance requirements
- Maintained as part of an evidence-based GRC process

All evidence should therefore be viewed as portfolio demonstration material rather than real organizational evidence.
