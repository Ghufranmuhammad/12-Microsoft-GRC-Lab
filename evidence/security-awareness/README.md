## 1. Purpose & Scope
This README documents the mandatory evidence artifacts retained within the enterprise Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) repository to substantiate the operational effectiveness and regulatory compliance of the Security Awareness Program during internal and external audits (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR).

---

## 2. Retained Evidence Artifacts

The following categories of evidence are systematically captured, archived, and secured within the GRC audit repository:

### Attendance & Completion Records
* **LMS Logs:** Automated database export files confirming course completion timestamps, employee IDs, and module titles for all mandatory onboarding and annual refresher programs.
* **Signed Attestations:** Digital sign-off records for policy acknowledgements and Code of Conduct rollouts.

### Assessment Results
* **Post-Training Quizzes:** Scorecards and pass/fail logs from end-of-module comprehension checks.
* **Privileged User Assessments:** Specialized technical evaluation records for high-risk system administrators.

### Phishing Simulation Results
* **Campaign Telemetry Exports:** Raw and aggregated CSV/XLSX reports detailing delivery, open, click, credential submission, and reporting rates per department and individual.
* **Phish-Alarm Button (PAB) Logs:** Timestamped metrics measuring the volume and speed of suspicious email reporting by end-users to the Security Operations Center (SOC).

### Communications
* **Awareness Campaigns:** Copies of broadcast security tips, monthly newsletters, and emergency phishing warnings distributed via corporate chat channels and intranet portals.
* **Escalation Notices:** Automated warning notifications sent to non-compliant personnel and their respective department managers prior to access restrictions.

### Training Material
* **Curriculum Repository:** Version-controlled slide decks, interactive video modules, micro-learning scripts, and phishing lure templates used throughout the reporting year.
* **Role-Based Handouts:** Specialized guidance documents distributed to Finance, HR, and Executive leadership regarding wire fraud and whaling defense.

### Metrics
* **KPI & KRI Registers:** Periodic executive summary dashboards tracking phishing failure rates, training completion percentages against SLAs, and remediation velocity.
* **Departmental Scorecards:** Trend analysis reports highlighting behavioral improvements or persistent risk hotspots across business units.

### Corrective Actions
* **Remedial Training Tickets:** ITSM/GRC tickets tracking the automatic assignment and completion of supplementary micro-learning modules for users failing phishing simulations.
* **Policy Exception Records:** Documented approvals signed by the CISO and HR Director for temporary training deadline extensions or risk acceptances.
