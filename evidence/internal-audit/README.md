# Internal Audit Evidence Folder

> **NOTICE:** This documentation and all associated artifacts pertain strictly to a **Fictional / Educational Audit Simulation**. All company names, systems, assets, and findings are simulated for training and instructional purposes only.

---

## 1. Audit Objective
The objective of this simulated internal audit evidence repository is to maintain a structured, transparent, and auditable record of all testing artifacts, working papers, identified control deficiencies, and corrective action plans gathered during the Information Security Management System (ISMS) evaluation.

## 2. Audit Scope
The scope encompasses all simulated operational units, cloud infrastructure environments, physical facilities, and administrative processes evaluated against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 benchmarks, internal organizational policies, and applicable UAE regulatory frameworks.

## 3. Evidence Folder Structure
This repository is organized into the following standardized directories:
* `evidence/internal-audit/README.md` — Overview of the evidence collection framework and audit lifecycle processes.
* `evidence/internal-audit/audit-plan/` — Audit charter, scheduling timelines, resource allocations, and scoping documents.
* `evidence/internal-audit/evidence-register/` — Master log of all requested, received, and reviewed evidentiary documents.
* `evidence/internal-audit/working-papers/` — Detailed testing working papers, control evaluation notes, and interview transcripts.
* `evidence/internal-audit/findings/` — Formal audit findings, risk ratings, root-cause analyses, and management responses.
* `evidence/internal-audit/corrective-actions/` — Corrective Action Plans (CAPs), remediation tracking logs, and retest verification records.

## 4. Testing Methodology
The audit follows a risk-based testing methodology aligned with professional internal auditing standards (IIA):
* **Scoping & Walkthroughs:** Preliminary discussions and process walkthroughs with simulated process owners.
* **Sample-Based Testing:** Inspection of system logs, policy documents, configuration exports, and historical records.
* **Evaluation:** Assessing control effectiveness as *Pass*, *Partial*, *Fail*, or *Not Tested*.
* **Validation:** Cross-referencing observed conditions against established control requirements and evidence.

## 5. Finding Process
When a control deficiency is identified:
1. A draft finding is logged in the working papers and reviewed with the auditee.
2. The risk is evaluated and assigned a severity rating (**High**, **Medium**, or **Low**).
3. Root-cause analysis is conducted to determine the underlying operational breakdown.
4. Formal corrective recommendations are established and presented in the final audit report.

## 6. Remediation Process
Following the publication of audit findings:
* Management formally responds and accepts or contests each finding.
* Designated remediation owners are assigned to implement corrective actions.
* Target completion dates are tracked via Corrective Action Plans (CAPs).
* Internal Audit performs follow-up testing and re-tests the controls to verify successful remediation and closure.
