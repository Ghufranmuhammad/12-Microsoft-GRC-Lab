## 1. Purpose & Scope
This README documents the mandatory evidence artifacts retained within the enterprise Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) audit repository. These records substantiate the operational effectiveness, oversight rigor, and formal decision-making processes of executive leadership and risk committees during internal and external regulatory audits (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR).

---

## 2. Retained Evidence Artifacts

The following categories of governance evidence are systematically captured, archived, and secured within the GRC audit repository:

### Committee Records
* **Charter & Mandate Documentation:** Approved governance charters outlining the scope, voting rights, and quorum requirements for the Enterprise Risk Committee, Audit Committee, and Privacy Steering Committee.
* **Attendance & Quorum Logs:** Signed participant registries verifying quorum compliance for all formal committee assemblies.

### Risk Acceptances
* **Formal Risk Waivers:** Signed risk acceptance forms documenting residual risk rationales, business justifications, compensating controls, and re-evaluation expiration dates approved by the CISO and risk owners.
* **Risk Register Sign-offs:** Version-controlled database exports capturing historical risk score modifications and formal sign-offs.

### Decision Log
* **Governance Decision Register:** Documented records of strategic determinations (e.g., mandatory FIDO2 hardware keys, automated onboarding gating, SIEM retention extensions) complete with background context, evaluated alternatives, approval signatures, and assigned deadlines.
* **Policy Exception Approvals:** Jointly signed exceptions authorizing temporary deviations from baseline security and training policies.

### Escalations
* **Escalation Notification Logs:** Timestamped audit trails recording triggered Level 2 and Level 3 escalations for critical risks, overdue remediations, repeated control failures, major incidents, and privacy breaches.
* **Crisis Management Briefings:** Executive status reports and communication transcripts delivered during active incident escalations.

### Meeting Evidence
* **Meeting Minutes & Transcripts:** Detailed records of discussions, minority opinions, and consensus agreements reached during quarterly GRC steering meetings.
* **Presentation Decks:** Version-controlled slide decks and briefing materials reviewed by executive committees prior to voting on major policy or architectural decisions.

### Action Tracking
* **GRC Remediation Tickets:** Integrated ITSM/GRC tracking tickets monitoring the end-to-end lifecycle of action items arising from audit findings, tabletop simulations, and committee directives.
* **Completion Verifications:** Evidence artifacts (e.g., configuration exports, test scripts, completion certificates) confirming the successful closure of assigned risk treatment tasks.
