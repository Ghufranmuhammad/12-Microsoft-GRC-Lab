# Management Escalation Procedure & Governance Framework

**Classification:** Confidential / Enterprise Governance & Risk  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Owner:** Enterprise Risk Management & GRC Steering Committee  

---

> **FICTIONAL / EDUCATIONAL SIMULATION**  
> *This escalation procedure document is produced exclusively for fictional and educational simulation purposes within a Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) framework.*

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This procedure defines the formal escalation pathways, response SLAs, and governance responsibilities for triggering executive intervention when enterprise risk thresholds are breached across risk management, audit, compliance, information security, vendor operations, privacy, and business continuity domains.

---

## 2. Escalation Triage Matrix
Issues are triaged into three operational levels based on severity and business impact:
* **Level 1 (Operational):** Managed directly by team leads and risk owners within standard operational SLAs.
* **Level 2 (Tactical):** Escalated to Department Heads and Risk Committee working groups when remediation deadlines are missed or thresholds approach critical limits.
* **Level 3 (Executive):** Immediate escalation to the CISO, Chief Executive Officer, and Enterprise Risk Committee for systemic, critical, or catastrophic failures.

---

## 3. Specific Domain Escalation Pathways

### Critical Risks
* **Trigger:** Identification of any risk with an inherent or residual score rated as Critical, or occurrence of critical vulnerabilities (e.g., unpatched CVSS >= 9.0) exceeding statutory SLAs.
* **Escalation Path:** Security Operations / Risk Lead $\rightarrow$ Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) $\rightarrow$ Enterprise Risk Committee.
* **Response SLA:** Immediate notification within **2 hours**; emergency mitigation sprint initiated within **24 hours**.

### Overdue Remediation
* **Trigger:** High-severity audit findings, control gaps, or risk treatment action items remaining un-remediated past their agreed-upon deadline by more than **30 calendar days**.
* **Escalation Path:** Internal Audit / GRC Lead $\rightarrow$ Business Unit Owner $\rightarrow$ Audit Committee & Executive Committee.
* **Response SLA:** Formal justification and revised recovery schedule submitted within **5 business days** of overdue status.

### Repeated Control Failures
* **Trigger:** A key enterprise control failing testing or automated monitoring assessments **2 or more consecutive times** within a rolling 12-month period.
* **Escalation Path:** GRC Testing Lead $\rightarrow$ Control Owner $\rightarrow$ Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) & Head of Internal Audit.
* **Response SLA:** Mandatory root-cause analysis and engineering re-design review convened within **10 business days**.

### Major Incidents
* **Trigger:** Security breaches, ransomware activations, data exfiltration events, or system outages classified as Severity 1 (Major/Catastrophic).
* **Escalation Path:** Incident Commander $\rightarrow$ Security Operations Center (SOC) Manager $\rightarrow$ Incident Response Team & Executive Crisis Committee.
* **Response SLA:** Executive briefing initiated within **1 hour** of incident confirmation.

### Vendor Weaknesses
* **Trigger:** Third-party vendor assessment revealing critical security gaps, failure to complete onboarding security reviews, or breach of contractual security SLAs.
* **Escalation Path:** Vendor Risk Manager $\rightarrow$ Procurement Lead $\rightarrow$ Chief Legal Officer & CISO.
* **Response SLA:** Formal vendor warning issued within **48 hours**; contract suspension or gating executed within **5 business days** if unaddressed.

### Privacy Concerns
* **Trigger:** Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) backlog exceeding statutory limits (>30 days), unauthorized cross-border data transfer, or potential personal data breach.
* **Escalation Path:** Data Protection Officer (DPO) $\rightarrow$ General Counsel & Privacy Steering Committee.
* **Response SLA:** Assessment and containment initiated within **24 hours**; regulatory notification evaluation completed within **72 hours** where mandated.

### Business Continuity Failures
* **Trigger:** Failure of scheduled disaster recovery/backup restoration drills, or actual invocation of business continuity plans due to infrastructure collapse or facility disruption.
* **Escalation Path:** Business Continuity Manager $\rightarrow$ Infrastructure Reliability Lead $\rightarrow$ Crisis Management Team.
* **Response SLA:** Immediate activation of secondary failover protocols; executive status report delivered every **4 hours** during active disruptions.
