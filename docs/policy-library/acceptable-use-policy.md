**Effective Date:** June 1, 2024  
**Review Date:** June 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Status:** Active  
**Policy Owner:** IT Operations Lead & CISO  
**Approver:** Director of IT & Executive Leadership Team  

---

## 1. Purpose
The Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines the mandatory rules, behavioral standards, and operational guidelines governing the use of all enterprise information technology resources, networks, devices, and communication systems. The objective of this policy is to protect our organization against security vulnerabilities, legal liabilities, and reputational damage while ensuring a productive, secure, and professional working environment for all employees, contractors, and authorized third parties.

---

## 2. Scope
This policy applies universally to all individuals who access, manage, or utilize enterprise IT assets, regardless of their physical location. Covered resources include corporate-issued hardware, bring-your-own-devices (BYOD), cloud applications, email systems, corporate networks, and internet access gateways.

---

## 3. Corporate Devices
* **Authorized Use:** Corporate-issued laptops, mobile phones, and tablets are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with job performance, violate security standards, or incur unauthorized costs.
* **Physical Security:** Users are strictly responsible for the physical security of their assigned corporate devices at all times. Laptops and mobile devices must never be left unattended in public places, unlocked vehicles, or unsecure environments.
* **Locking and Encryption:** Devices must be locked immediately whenever left unattended (using `Win + L` or equivalent screen locks). Full-disk encryption (BitLocker / FileVault) must remain enabled and active on all corporate endpoints.

---

## 4. Email & Communication
* **Professional Conduct:** Corporate email, messaging platforms (e.g., Microsoft Teams, Slack), and collaboration tools must be used in a professional, ethical, and lawful manner.
* **Prohibited Content:** Transmitting defamatory, harassing, sexually explicit, racist, or fraudulent content through corporate communication channels is strictly prohibited.
* **Phishing Vigilance:** Users must exercise extreme caution when opening external emails, clicking embedded links, or downloading attachments. Any suspicious message must be reported immediately using the integrated phishing reporting button.

---

## 5. Internet Access
* **Business-Centric Usage:** Internet access via corporate networks and VPNs is intended for business activities. Incidental personal web browsing is permitted during breaks, provided it does not violate browsing restrictions.
* **Restricted Content:** Accessing websites containing adult content, illegal file sharing (torrenting), gambling, hate speech, malware distribution, or unauthorized streaming services is strictly prohibited.
* **Traffic Filtering:** All internet traffic is filtered and monitored by enterprise secure web gateways to detect malicious payloads, command-and-control communication, and policy infractions.

---

## 6. Cloud Services & Storage
* **Approved SaaS Platforms:** Employees may only store, process, or transmit enterprise data using company-approved cloud platforms (e.g., Microsoft 365, OneDrive, approved corporate SharePoint repositories).
* **Shadow IT Prohibition:** Utilizing unauthorized public cloud storage, consumer file-sharing services, or unvetted generative AI tools for enterprise work is strictly prohibited.
* **Data Classification:** Cloud-stored documents must be properly labeled and restricted based on our data classification standards (Internal, Confidential, Restricted).

---

## 7. Passwords & Authentication
* **Password Hygiene:** Passwords must be complex, meeting minimum length (12+ characters) and complexity requirements (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols). Reusing corporate passwords on external personal accounts is strictly prohibited.
* **Credential Secrecy:** Passwords, PINs, and authentication keys must never be shared with anyone, including managers, IT support, or colleagues. Passwords must never be written down on sticky notes or stored in unencrypted plaintext files.

---

## 8. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
* **Mandatory Enrollment:** Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all user accounts accessing corporate networks, email, VPNs, and cloud applications.
* **Authentication Prompts:** Users must verify their identity using approved authenticator apps or hardware tokens. Approving unsolicited or unrecognized MFA push notifications is strictly prohibited and must be reported immediately to the SOC.

---

## 9. Software Installation
* **Administrator Privileges:** End users do not possess local administrator rights on corporate endpoints. Installing unapproved software, freeware, shareware, or browser extensions is strictly prohibited.
* **Vetting and Deployment:** All software requests must be submitted through the IT Service Desk. Software is vetted for security vulnerabilities, licensing compliance, and compatibility prior to centralized deployment.

---

## 10. Removable Media
* **USB and Storage Controls:** The use of unauthorized USB flash drives, external hard drives, and removable media is restricted across corporate endpoints.
* **Encryption Requirement:** When approved by IT for business transfer, removable media must utilize hardware-level AES-256 encryption. Storing unencrypted Confidential enterprise data on personal USB drives is strictly prohibited.

---

## 11. Personal Devices (BYOD)
* **Enrollment Mandate:** Personal smartphones and laptops used to access corporate email or cloud resources must be enrolled in our Mobile Device Management (MDM) / Microsoft Intune platform.
* **Security Controls:** BYOD devices must enforce PIN/biometric locks, automatic screen timeout, and remote wipe capabilities. IT reserves the right to wipe corporate data from personal devices upon employment termination or security compromise.

---

## 12. Remote Work & Home Offices
* **Secure Wi-Fi:** Home and remote workspaces must utilize secure, encrypted Wi-Fi networks (WPA2/WPA3 with strong passphrases). Default router administrator credentials must be changed.
* **VPN Requirement:** When accessing internal enterprise systems from outside the corporate office, employees must connect via the authorized corporate VPN gateway.
* **Visual Privacy:** Remote workers must position screens to prevent family members, roommates, or visitors from viewing confidential enterprise data. Physical documents must be shredded securely.

---

## 13. Prohibited Activities
The following activities are strictly prohibited across all enterprise IT environments:
* Attempting to bypass security controls, firewalls, DLP filters, or access restrictions.
* Conducting unauthorized vulnerability scans, penetration tests, or network sniffing.
* Introducing malware, ransomware, logic bombs, or malicious code onto enterprise networks.
* Engaging in cryptocurrency mining using corporate compute resources or cloud workloads.
* Falsifying identity or impersonating another user in communications or system logins.

---

## 14. Monitoring & Privacy
* **Right to Monitor:** Users should have no expectation of privacy when utilizing corporate IT resources, networks, email, or devices. All activities are subject to real-time logging, monitoring, and inspection by authorized security personnel.
* **Lawful Oversight:** Monitoring is conducted to ensure operational continuity, prevent security breaches, investigate policy violations, and maintain compliance with UAE legal requirements.

---

## 15. Reporting Violations & Enforcement
* **Reporting Obligation:** Any employee who witnesses or suspects a violation of this AUP must report it immediately to the IT Service Desk or the Security Operations Center (SOC).
* **Disciplinary Action:** Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy will result in a thorough investigation and may lead to disciplinary action, including formal reprimands, revocation of system privileges, suspension, termination of employment, and potential legal prosecution.
