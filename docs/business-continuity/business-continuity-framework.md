# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Framework

## 1. Overview
This document establishes the enterprise Business Continuity Management (BCM) framework. It defines the structured lifecycle required to identify organizational risks, assess operational impacts, design resilient recovery strategies, and maintain continuous improvement across all business and IT functions.

---

## 2. Core Program Components
* **Business Continuity Management (BCM):** The overarching governance structure ensuring organizational resilience against disruptive incidents.
* **Business Impact Analysis (BIA):** The systematic process used to determine the criticality of business functions and the impact of operational disruptions.
* **Risk Assessment:** Identifying vulnerabilities, threat scenarios, and the likelihood of disruptions.
* **Recovery Strategies:** Alternative operating procedures and resource requirements established to maintain critical services during an outage.
* **Business Continuity Plan (BCP):** Actionable procedures for maintaining business operations during a crisis.
* **Disaster Recovery (DRP):** Technical procedures focused on restoring IT infrastructure, cloud tenants, and data systems.
* **Crisis Management:** Executive leadership structure and communication protocols during severe disruptions.
* **Testing & Exercises:** Periodic simulations to validate recovery capabilities and staff readiness.
* **Lessons Learned & Continuous Improvement:** Post-incident and post-test reviews driving ongoing framework enhancements.

## 3. Business Continuity Lifecycle
The following step-by-step lifecycle governs the continuous evaluation and hardening of the organization's resilience posture:

### Lifecycle Stage Breakdown:
1. **Risk Assessment:** Identify external and internal threats (e.g., cyberattacks, infrastructure failures, natural disasters) and evaluate baseline exposures.
2. **Business Impact Analysis (BIA):** Quantify operational and financial downtime impacts to determine Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption (MTPD).
3. **Critical Services:** Isolate mission-critical business processes, supporting applications, and dependent third-party vendors.
4. **Recovery Requirements:** Define precise Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) for each critical service.
5. **Recovery Strategy:** Formulate technical and operational strategies (e.g., hot site failover, secondary data backups, manual workarounds).
6. **BCP / DRP:** Author comprehensive, documented Business Continuity Plans and Disaster Recovery Plans detailing roles, responsibilities, and step-by-step execution.
7. **Testing:** Conduct annual tabletop exercises, component failovers, and full-scale simulations to validate plan effectiveness.
8. **Lessons Learned:** Analyze test results and actual incident post-mortems to pinpoint gaps, communication bottlenecks, and control failures.
9. **Improvement:** Update plans, retrain staff, and adjust technical controls to continuously elevate organizational readiness.
