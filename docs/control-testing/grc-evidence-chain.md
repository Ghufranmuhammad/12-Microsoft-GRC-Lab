# GRC Evidence Chain Lifecycle

Business Risk  
↓  
Requirement  
↓  
Control  
↓  
Control Owner  
↓  
Evidence  
↓  
Testing  
↓  
Finding  
↓  
Corrective Action  
↓  
Retest  
↓  
Closure  

---

### Stage Explanations

1. **Business Risk:** Identifies the potential threat or vulnerability that could negatively impact organizational objectives, data security, or regulatory compliance.
2. **Requirement:** Translates high-level risk statements into mandatory legal, regulatory, or internal policy obligations that the organization must satisfy.
3. **Control:** Implements a technical, operational, or administrative safeguard designed to mitigate the identified risk and fulfill compliance requirements.
4. **Control Owner:** Assigns accountability to a specific team or individual responsible for maintaining, monitoring, and operating the control.
5. **Evidence:** Collects verifiable artifacts, logs, or configurations that prove whether the control is operating effectively.
6. **Testing:** Executes structured verification procedures and evaluates collected evidence against expected results.
7. **Finding:** Documents the final test outcome, noting any exceptions, control deficiencies, or failures discovered during evaluation.
8. **Corrective Action:** Formulates and implements a remediation plan to fix underlying root causes when a control fails testing.
9. **Retest:** Re-evaluates the remediated environment using fresh evidence to ensure the weakness has been successfully resolved.
10. **Closure:** Formally concludes the audit loop by signing off on the remediated control and recording the completed lifecycle.
