# Internal Audit Charter: Information Security

## Audit Title
Information Security Internal Audit — Fictional UAE Company

---

## 1. Audit Objective
The primary objective of this internal audit is to independently and objectively evaluate the design, operating effectiveness, and maturity of the organization's Information Security Management System (ISMS). This audit assesses whether information assets are adequately protected, risks are appropriately managed, and operational controls comply with established internal policies and applicable regulatory mandates.

## 2. Audit Scope
The audit encompasses all operational units, technical systems, cloud environments, and physical facilities of the organization. Specifically, the scope evaluates the following domains:
* **Information Security Governance:** Policies, procedures, management oversight, and security leadership structures.
* **Risk Management:** Identification, assessment, treatment, and ongoing monitoring of organizational and third-party risks.
* **Access Control:** User provisioning, privileged access management, multi-factor authentication (MFA), and periodic access reviews.
* **Data Classification:** Handling, storage, transmission, labeling, and lifecycle management of sensitive and personal data.
* **Incident Management:** Incident response plans, detection mechanisms, containment procedures, and breach notification timelines.
* **Business Continuity:** Business Impact Analysis (BIA), Business Continuity Plans (BCP), Disaster Recovery (DRP), and testing cadences.
* **Vendor Risk Management:** Third-party onboarding, due diligence, security questionnaires, and ongoing vendor monitoring.
* **Security Awareness:** Employee training programs, phishing simulations, and security culture metrics.

## 3. Audit Criteria
The assessment and testing of controls will be measured against the following authoritative benchmarks:
* **ISO/IEC 27001:2022:** Information security management systems requirements.
* **Internal Policies:** The organization's formal information security policies, standard operating procedures, and risk appetite statements.
* **Regulatory & Legal Frameworks:** Applicable United Arab Emirates (UAE) data protection laws, cybersecurity regulations, and sectoral compliance standards.

## 4. Audit Period
* **Review Window:** Operations, logs, and control samples evaluated cover the preceding 12 months (August 2025 – August 2026).
* **Execution Dates:** Fieldwork and testing conducted between September 1, 2026, and September 25, 2026.

## 5. Audit Team
* **Lead Internal Auditor:** Senior GRC & Compliance Specialist
* **IT Security Auditor:** Cloud Infrastructure & Security Engineer
* **Compliance Analyst:** Data Protection & Regulatory Specialist

## 6. Audit Sponsor
* **Audit Sponsor:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) & Executive Management Board

## 7. Audited Departments
* Information Technology & Cloud Infrastructure
* Information Security / SecOps
* Human Resources & Talent Management
* Legal & Compliance
* Procurement & Vendor Management
* Customer Operations & Support

## 8. Methodology
The internal audit follows a risk-based auditing methodology aligned with professional internal auditing standards (IIA):
* **Planning & Preliminary Review:** Scoping, document requests, and walkthrough interviews with process owners.
* **Control Testing:** Sample-based testing of technical configurations, policy documents, audit logs, and access reviews.
* **Evaluation & Finding Development:** Analyzing control deficiencies, determining root causes, and rating associated risks.
* **Reporting & Remediation:** Publishing the audit report, assigning remedial owners, and tracking corrective action plans (CAPs).

## 9. Limitations
* **Scope Limitations:** Testing is restricted to documented systems, sampled records, and active cloud tenants within the defined audit period. Unforeseen legacy shadow IT systems outside registered asset inventories are excluded unless identified during fieldwork.
* **Inherent Limitations:** Audit testing provides reasonable, but not absolute, assurance due to the inherent limitations of internal control systems and sampling methodologies.

## 10. Confidentiality Statement
All information, documentation, system access logs, interviews, and working papers gathered during the course of this internal audit are strictly confidential. Data will be handled in accordance with corporate data protection policies and used solely for the purpose of executing this audit and improving the organization's security posture.
