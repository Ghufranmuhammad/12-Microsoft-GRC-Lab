## Overall Security/GRC Posture

### Moderate

DesertTech Solutions has established several important security and GRC processes, including access management, MFA, security awareness, data classification, DLP, vendor assessments, incident response and backup management.

However, the posture remains Moderate because several controls require improvement and some activities are not consistently supported by evidence.

The main concern is not the complete absence of security controls. It is inconsistent implementation, testing and monitoring.

## Top 3 Risks

### 1. Backup Recovery Risk — Critical

Critical systems have not consistently undergone successful restore testing. A backup that has never been tested cannot provide management with strong assurance that the organization can recover after ransomware, system failure or data corruption.

### 2. Privileged Access Risk — High

Privileged accounts require strong authentication and periodic review. Any privileged account without MFA or without appropriate review creates a potentially significant path to unauthorized access.

### 3. Vulnerability Remediation Risk — High

Overdue critical vulnerabilities increase the likelihood that attackers could exploit known weaknesses.

## Most Concerning KRI

### Failed or Missing Backup Restore Tests

This is the most concerning KRI because it directly affects business resilience.

The organization may have backups, but management needs evidence that critical systems can actually be restored within approved recovery objectives.

## Biggest Control Weakness

The biggest weakness is inconsistent evidence and control testing.

Several controls may exist on paper, but GRC assurance depends on proving that controls are operating effectively.

## Most Urgent Remediation

Complete restore testing for all critical systems.

Management should confirm:

- Which systems are critical.
- Required recovery objectives.
- Whether backups are complete.
- Whether restoration succeeds.
- Whether recovery objectives are achieved.
- Whether failed tests generate corrective actions.

## Management Recommendation — Next 30 Days

Management should prioritize five actions:

1. Complete critical-system backup restore testing.
2. Verify MFA coverage for every privileged account.
3. Review and remediate critical overdue vulnerabilities.
4. Complete outstanding high-risk vendor assessments.
5. Establish consistent monthly GRC evidence and control-review reporting.

The objective should not simply be to increase dashboard scores. The objective should be to reduce actual business risk and provide management with reliable evidence that important controls are working.
