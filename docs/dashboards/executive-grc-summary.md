# Executive GRC Summary

## Executive Assessment

DesertTech Solutions has established a foundation for managing cybersecurity and compliance risk. Key processes exist across access management, data protection, security awareness, incident response, vendor management and business continuity.

However, the current environment should be considered **Moderate Risk** because some important controls are not consistently tested, monitored or supported by sufficient evidence.

## Key Risks

The three most important areas requiring management attention are:

### 1. Business Recovery

Backup procedures exist, but recovery testing must demonstrate that critical systems can actually be restored when needed.

### 2. Privileged Access

Privileged accounts require strong authentication and regular review. Any gap in this area could provide an attacker with significant access to business systems.

### 3. Vulnerability Management

Known security weaknesses must be addressed within defined timeframes. Delayed remediation increases exposure to preventable attacks.

## Management Priorities

During the next 30 days, management should prioritize:

- Testing recovery of critical systems.
- Confirming strong authentication for all privileged accounts.
- Resolving overdue critical vulnerabilities.
- Completing assessments of high-risk suppliers.
- Strengthening evidence collection and control monitoring.

## Business Impact

These actions will improve the organization's ability to prevent security incidents, detect problems earlier, recover from disruption and demonstrate compliance to customers, auditors and other stakeholders.

## Board-Level Message

The organization has the necessary building blocks for a mature GRC program, but management should focus on **consistent execution and evidence**, not simply the existence of policies and technologies.

The immediate objective should be to reduce the highest business risks while strengthening management's confidence that critical controls are operating effectively.

**Overall Direction: Improve control effectiveness, reduce material risk, and strengthen evidence-based assurance.**
