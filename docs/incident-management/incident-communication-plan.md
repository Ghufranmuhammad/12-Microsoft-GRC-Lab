# Incident Communication Plan

## 1. Internal Stakeholders

### Employees
* **Objective:** Ensure staff safety, prevent accidental data spillage, maintain operational awareness, and provide clear instruction on what actions to take or avoid.
* **Channels:** Internal corporate email, Slack/Teams broadcast channels, intranet banner alerts, and emergency SMS broadcast (for life-safety or critical remote work disruptions).
* **Key Content:** High-level summary of the incident (without compromising security), immediate behavioral expectations (e.g., password resets, reporting suspicious emails), and helpdesk contact details.
* **Timing:** Initial notification within 1 hour of severity confirmation for major incidents; regular updates as containment progresses.

### Management & Executive Leadership
* **Objective:** Enable informed business decision-making, resource allocation, and risk management oversight.
* **Channels:** Executive briefing calls, secure encrypted messaging channels, and concise written incident status summaries.
* **Key Content:** Business impact assessment, operational downtime estimates, containment status, reputational risk analysis, and resource or budget requests.
* **Timing:** Within 30 minutes of severe incident classification (SEV-1/SEV-2); progress updates every 2 to 4 hours until resolution.

### IT & Security Teams
* **Objective:** Coordinate technical containment, forensic preservation, and system remediation efforts.
* **Channels:** Dedicated incident bridge (audio/video conference), dedicated Slack/Teams war room channels, and ticketing system (Jira/ServiceNow).
* **Key Content:** Technical telemetry, log analysis, Indicator of Compromise (IoC) sharing, containment execution steps, and patch verification.
* **Timing:** Immediate upon alert dispatch (24/7 continuous operational cadence).

---

## 2. Specialized Internal & External Partners

### Legal & Privacy Teams
* **Objective:** Protect organizational legal standing, evaluate liability, and manage regulatory exposure.
* **Channels:** Secure, privileged communication channels (encrypted email, direct phone briefings).
* **Key Content:** Nature of compromised data, regulatory jurisdictions involved, third-party contractual obligations, and drafts of external communications.
* **Timing:** Immediately upon confirming potential data exposure or legal liability thresholds.

### Vendors & Third-Party Partners
* **Objective:** Coordinate joint response efforts if third-party software, cloud infrastructure, or managed services are implicated.
* **Channels:** Vendor security operations center (SOC) escalation contacts, official vendor support ticketing portals, and joint incident bridges.
* **Key Content:** Vulnerability details, API access revocation requests, log exchange, and patch deployment coordination.
* **Timing:** Concurrent with internal triage if third-party systems are involved.

---

## 3. External Stakeholders & Authorities

### Regulators (Where Applicable)
* **Objective:** Comply with statutory data breach notification laws and maintain regulatory transparency.
* **Channels:** Official regulatory reporting portals, secure encrypted submission forms, or direct legal counsel liaison.
* **Key Content:** Description of the incident, categories of data affected, estimated number of impacted individuals, remediation steps taken, and designated point of contact.
* **Timing:** *Requires verification with legal counsel and compliance officers based on specific jurisdictional mandates and applicable statutory reporting timelines.*

### Customers & End-Users (Where Applicable)
* **Objective:** Maintain trust, provide transparent guidance on protective measures (e.g., credential changes), and fulfill contractual disclosure obligations.
* **Channels:** Direct email notification, public status pages, customer support talking points, and web portal notices.
* **Key Content:** What happened, what information was involved, what steps the organization has taken, what actions the customer should take, and dedicated support contact channels.
* **Timing:** Following legal and management sign-off, typically coordinated alongside regulatory notifications or public disclosures.
