# Incident Response Policy

1. **Incident definition** – Clear criteria establishing what constitutes a security incident versus a standard operational anomaly.
2. **Reporting obligations** – Mandatory timelines and channels for employees and IT staff to report suspected security breaches.
3. **Roles and responsibilities** – Defined duties for incident commanders, system administrators, legal counsel, and communication teams.
4. **Severity levels** – Tiered categorization (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Critical) based on operational impact, data sensitivity, and scope.
5. **Escalation** – Procedures for notifying senior leadership and specialized response teams when an incident exceeds pre-defined thresholds.
6. **Communication** – Protocols for internal stakeholder updates and external public relations or customer notifications.
7. **Evidence handling** – Strict guidelines for maintaining chain of custody, capturing forensic snapshots, and securing digital artifacts.
8. **Regulatory/legal escalation** – Mandatory compliance steps for reporting data breaches to relevant legal authorities and regulatory bodies.
9. **Post-incident review** – Post-mortem requirements to analyze response effectiveness and document lessons learned.
10. **Policy review** – Periodic annual or post-incident review schedules to update policies based on evolving threat landscapes.
