# Incident Response Methodology

1. **Preparation** – Establishing policies, training staff, deploying security tools, and maintaining incident response plans.
2. **Detection and reporting** – Identifying anomalous activity through monitoring systems, alerts, or user reporting mechanisms.
3. **Triage** – Initial assessment to verify the validity of the alert, determine scope, and prioritize severity.
4. **Classification** – Categorizing the incident type (e.g., malware, unauthorized access, data exfiltration) to drive appropriate playbooks.
5. **Containment** – Isolating affected systems to prevent lateral movement or further damage.
6. **Eradication** – Removing threat vectors, malware, and closing vulnerabilities exploited during the incident.
7. **Recovery** – Restoring systems to normal operational status and verifying integrity.
8. **Lessons learned** – Post-incident review meetings to identify root causes and improve future defenses.
9. **Evidence preservation** – Maintaining chain of custody and securely logging artifacts for potential forensic or legal review.
10. **Management reporting** – Briefing stakeholders, leadership, and regulatory bodies on impact, remediation, and preventive measures.
