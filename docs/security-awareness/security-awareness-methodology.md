# Security Awareness Methodology & Governance Framework

**Classification:** Confidential / Enterprise Security Governance  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Owner:** Enterprise Security Awareness & Human Risk Management Team  

---

> **FICTIONAL / EDUCATIONAL SIMULATION**  
> *This methodology document, along with all associated training records, simulation metrics, and compliance logs, is produced exclusively for fictional and educational simulation purposes within a Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) framework.*

---

## 1. Program Objectives
The enterprise security awareness program aims to cultivate a security-first culture, shifting the organizational paradigm from passive compliance to active risk mitigation. Core objectives include:
* Empowering employees to recognize, resist, and report social engineering attacks, credential harvesting, and phishing attempts.
* Establishing measurable behavioral baselines to reduce human-vector vulnerabilities across all business units.
* Ensuring continuous adherence to regulatory compliance mandates regarding security education and privacy awareness.

---

## 2. Audience Identification
Training modules and awareness campaigns are segmented by role-based risk profiles to ensure relevance and appropriate depth:
* **General Workforce:** All full-time, part-time, and contractor personnel receiving baseline onboarding and annual security refresher curricula.
* **Privileged Access Users:** System administrators, DevOps engineers, and database owners receiving advanced technical threat training and secure handling guidelines.
* **Executive Leadership & Board Members:** High-profile personnel targeted with specialized modules focusing on executive spear-phishing (whaling), social engineering, and incident disclosure protocols.

---

## 3. Training Requirements
Mandatory curriculum milestones are enforced across all employment lifecycles:
* **Onboarding SLA:** New hires must complete core security and privacy awareness modules within 14 calendar days of provisioning credentials.
* **Annual Refresher:** All active personnel must complete the annual security recertification curriculum during the designated Q3 enterprise campaign window.
* **Remedial Assignments:** Personnel failing simulated phishing exercises or exhibiting high-risk behavior are automatically assigned supplementary micro-learning modules within 48 hours.

---

## 4. Risk-based Training
Curricula dynamically adapt to emerging threat intelligence and historical behavioral data:
* **Targeted Interventions:** High-risk departments (e.g., Finance, Human Resources, Procurement) receive specialized modules focusing on Business Email Compromise (BEC) and invoice fraud.
* **Adaptive Phishing Simulation:** Employees who repeatedly fall for simulated credential harvesting are placed on a higher-frequency simulation cadence.

---

## 5. Awareness Channels
Multiple communication vectors maintain persistent security visibility across the hybrid workspace:
* **Learning Management System (LMS):** Centralized repository for structured interactive courses and tracking certificates.
* **Secure Collaboration Channels:** Automated weekly security tips, threat advisories, and red-flag alerts broadcast via dedicated corporate chat channels.
* **Intranet & Digital Signage:** Periodic security spotlights, phishing warning banners, and zero-trust reminders displayed across corporate portals.

---

## 6. Frequency
Campaign execution operates on structured periodic cycles:
* **Continuous:** Automated phishing simulations deployed monthly on randomized schedules.
* **Monthly:** Micro-learning capsules (3–5 minutes) delivered to reinforce high-priority security concepts.
* **Quarterly & Annual:** Comprehensive compliance tracking rollups and full curriculum refreshes.

---

## 7. Completion Tracking
Accountability is maintained through automated tracking pipelines integrated with enterprise identity platforms:
* **Automated Sync:** LMS connectors link directly with HR directory feeds to track active status and assign modules dynamically.
* **Escalation Workflow:** Automated warning notifications are sent to managers at 7 days and 3 days prior to training deadlines. Non-compliance results in automated account access restrictions until completion.

---

## 8. Testing
Human risk validation is evaluated through controlled, non-destructive testing methodologies:
* **Simulated Phishing Campaigns:** Multi-vector simulations (email, SMS/Smishing, QR code/Quishing) measuring employee click rates, credential submissions, and reporting velocity.
* **Phish-Alarm Button (PAB):** Tracking the volume and speed of suspicious email reporting by end-users to the Security Operations Center (SOC).

---

## 9. Metrics
Program effectiveness is quantified using standardized Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and Key Risk Indicators (KRIs):
* **Phishing Failure Rate:** Percentage of employees who interact with or submit credentials during simulations.
* **Reporting Rate:** Percentage of users who actively report simulated or live phishing emails using the PAB tool.
* **Training Completion Rate:** Proportion of active employees completing assigned modules within statutory SLAs (Target: $\ge$ 95.0%).

---

## 10. Continuous Improvement
The awareness program undergoes regular optimization based on threat landscape shifts and empirical data:
* **Monthly Retrospectives:** Security awareness leads review simulation metrics and feedback to refine lure designs and training scripts.
* **Annual Curriculum Overhaul:** Course content is updated annually to incorporate new attack vectors (e.g., deepfake voice/video social engineering, AI-driven phishing lures).
