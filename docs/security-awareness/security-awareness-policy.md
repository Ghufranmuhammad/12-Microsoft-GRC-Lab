# Enterprise Security Awareness Policy

**Classification:** Confidential / Enterprise Security Governance  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Owner:** Enterprise Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) & HR Compliance  

---

> **FICTIONAL / EDUCATIONAL SIMULATION**  
> *This corporate policy document is produced exclusively for fictional and educational simulation purposes within a Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) framework.*

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy establishes mandatory security awareness and training requirements for all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors operating enterprise systems or handling corporate data, ensuring alignment with regulatory standards and risk mitigation frameworks.

---

## 2. Mandatory Training
Completion of designated security awareness and privacy modules is a mandatory condition of employment. All personnel must complete assigned coursework within established statutory timeframes.

---

## 3. New-Joiner Training
All newly onboarded personnel must complete core security and privacy awareness orientation modules within **14 calendar days** of receiving system credentials. Network access privileges are conditionally restricted until completion is verified via LMS API sync.

---

## 4. Annual Refresher
All active personnel must complete the annual security recertification curriculum during the designated Q3 enterprise campaign window to maintain active network credentials.

---

## 5. Role-Based Training
Curricula are tailored to organizational risk profiles:
* **Privileged Users:** System administrators and DevOps engineers must complete advanced technical security modules annually.
* **High-Risk Business Units:** Finance, Procurement, and HR personnel undergo targeted training regarding Business Email Compromise (BEC) and wire fraud.

---

## 6. Phishing Simulations
The organization conducts continuous, randomized simulated phishing and multi-channel social engineering exercises to evaluate human risk resilience. Personnel failing simulations are automatically assigned immediate just-in-time micro-training.

---

## 7. Reporting Obligations
All personnel are strictly required to report suspicious emails, phishing attempts, or security anomalies immediately using the integrated Phish-Alarm Button (PAB) or by notifying the Security Operations Center (SOC).

---

## 8. Exceptions
Formal exceptions to training deadlines or module requirements must be submitted in writing and require joint written approval from the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and the Director of Human Resources.

---

## 9. Records
Training completion certificates, simulation telemetry logs, and exception records are maintained in the enterprise Learning Management System (LMS) audit logs in accordance with data retention schedules.

---

## 10. Enforcement
Non-compliance with mandatory training deadlines or recurring simulation failures triggers automated management escalations and potential revocation of corporate network access until remediation is achieved.

---

## 11. Review
This policy is reviewed annually by the Enterprise Risk Committee and updated to reflect emerging threat landscapes and regulatory changes.
