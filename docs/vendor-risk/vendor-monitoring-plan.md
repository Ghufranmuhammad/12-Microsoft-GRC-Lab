# Ongoing Vendor Monitoring Plan

## 1. Annual Reassessment
All active Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors undergo a full mandatory security posture reassessment annually, including questionnaire updates and third-party document collection.

## 2. Security Incidents & Threat Intelligence
Real-time monitoring of security advisories, vulnerability disclosures, and public breach notifications affecting active vendors via automated threat intelligence feeds.

## 3. Certificate Expiry Tracking
Automated tracking of SOC 2, ISO 27001, and insurance policy expiration dates with alerts triggered 60, 30, and 15 days prior to lapse.

## 4. SLA Performance Reviews
Quarterly operational reviews analyzing vendor uptime, support ticket resolution times, and system availability metrics against established service level agreements.

## 5. Vulnerability & Security Changes
Continuous review of vendor security posture updates, significant patch releases, and notification of major software architecture modifications.

## 6. Material Changes
Mandatory vendor reporting of corporate restructuring, mergers, acquisitions, or shifts in offshore data processing locations.

## 7. Contract Renewal Checkpoints
Comprehensive 90-day review preceding any automatic contract renewal or multi-year agreement extension to re-verify business justification and risk posture.
