# Vendor Tiering Framework

## Tier 1: Critical
- **Criteria:** Access to restricted core infrastructure, sensitive personal data (PII/PHI), financial records, or services essential to daily operations where downtime immediately halts business.
- **Examples:** Cloud IaaS providers, Core ERP, Identity Providers, Payroll processors.
- **Assessment Frequency:** **Annual** full comprehensive re-assessment, mandatory annual SOC 2 / ISO review, and continuous monitoring.

## Tier 2: High
- **Criteria:** Access to confidential internal data or supporting business systems that, if compromised, would cause operational disruption or moderate reputational damage.
- **Examples:** Customer support ticketing systems, Vulnerability scanners, HR recruitment platforms.
- **Assessment Frequency:** **Annual** security questionnaire review and third-party certificate validation.

## Tier 3: Medium
- **Criteria:** Limited access to internal networks, handling non-sensitive or aggregated data with minimal operational disruption risk.
- **Examples:** Marketing automation tools, BI dashboards, PDF collaboration suites.
- **Assessment Frequency:** **Biennial (Every 2 Years)** lightweight questionnaire and policy review.

## Tier 4: Low
- **Criteria:** No direct access to internal network or corporate data; public-facing or basic office utility services.
- **Examples:** Office supply vendors, static software license providers.
- **Assessment Frequency:** **Triennial (Every 3 Years)** baseline intake review and lightweight screening.
