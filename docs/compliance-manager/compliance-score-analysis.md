## What Does the Score Tell Management?
The Compliance Manager score provides a high-level, quantitative indicator of **progress against Microsoft’s recommended implementation tasks**. It shows leadership:
* Which foundational technical controls have been successfully deployed.
* Where technical gaps exist across key risk areas (e.g., backup testing scoring 0%).
* Trend analysis over time as improvement actions are remediated.

## What Does It NOT Tell Management?
As explicitly warned in Microsoft's official documentation, **a high Compliance Manager score does not mean an organization is fully compliant or immune to security breaches.** 
* **It measures tool configuration, not absolute security effectiveness:** Just because a technical control is toggled on does not mean it is configured correctly or monitored properly.
* **It ignores non-technical external controls:** Many compliance standards (like ISO 27001 or SOC 2) require physical security checks, operational governance, and legal agreements that Microsoft Purview cannot automatically evaluate.
* **Point weighting is standardized:** Points are assigned by Microsoft based on their risk assessment model, which may not align perfectly with your company's unique risk appetite or specific regulatory mandates.

> **Interview Talking Point:** "When presenting to executives, I always clarify that Compliance Manager measures *control implementation progress within Microsoft's ecosystem*, not overall legal or regulatory compliance. A 95% score is a great operational metric, but it requires qualitative context, risk assessments, and independent audits to validate true compliance posture."
