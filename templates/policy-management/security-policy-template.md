## 2. Purpose
* State clearly why this policy exists and what business, operational, or security objectives it serves.
* Define how this policy protects the organization's information assets, brand reputation, and operational continuity against evolving threats.
* Align organizational security goals with statutory, regulatory, and contractual obligations (e.g., ISO 27001, NIST CSF, NCA ECC, UAE PDPL).

---

## 3. Scope
* Define the exact boundaries of applicability across the enterprise.
* Specify whether the policy applies to all employees, contractors, third-party vendors, consultants, and partner entities.
* Identify all covered technological environments (e.g., cloud workloads, on-premises data centers, mobile devices, remote work infrastructure, SaaS platforms).

## 4. Definitions
| Term / Acronym | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Asset** | Any data, hardware, software, facility, or personnel critical to enterprise operations. |
| **Compensating Control** | An alternative security measure designed to satisfy the requirement of a security control that cannot be directly implemented. |
| **Information Security Management System (ISMS)** | A systematic approach to managing sensitive company information so that it remains secure. |
| **Personal Data** | Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person under UAE PDPL mandates. |
| **Risk** | The effect of uncertainty on objectives, measured in terms of likelihood and impact. |

---

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
* **Board of Directors / Executive Leadership:** Ultimate accountability for enterprise risk management, resource allocation, and policy governance approval.
* **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO):** Responsible for designing, implementing, and overseeing the enterprise security policy framework and monitoring compliance.
* **Policy Owners / Department Heads:** Responsible for drafting, maintaining, and operationalizing domain-specific policies within their business units.
* **All Employees & Contractors:** Required to read, acknowledge, and adhere to all applicable security policies during the course of employment.
* **Internal Audit / GRC Team:** Responsible for independently reviewing policy adherence, collecting audit evidence, and tracking corrective actions.

---

## 6. Policy Statements
* **6.1 Core Mandate:** All enterprise assets must be handled in strict accordance with their assigned classification and security criticality.
* **6.2 Least Privilege Access:** Access to enterprise systems, networks, and data repositories must be restricted based strictly on the principle of least privilege and legitimate business need.
* **6.3 Protection of Information:** Data at rest and in transit must be protected using robust cryptographic algorithms (e.g., AES-256, TLS 1.3) approved by the security team.
* **6.4 Incident Reporting:** Any suspected security incident, data breach, or policy violation must be reported immediately to the Security Operations Center (SOC).

---

## 7. Exceptions
* Any deviation from this policy requires formal approval via the Enterprise Policy Exception Process.
* Exceptions must include a documented business justification, risk assessment, defined compensating controls, and a strict time-bound expiry date.
* Permanent exceptions are strictly prohibited. All exceptions must be reviewed at least annually.

---

## 8. Compliance
* Compliance with this policy is mandatory across all business units and subsidiaries.
* Regular compliance audits, automated control assessments, and independent reviews will be conducted by the GRC team to verify operational adherence.
* Failure to comply may result in audit findings, corrective action plans (CAPAs), and potential operational restrictions.

---

## 9. Enforcement
* Violations of this policy will be investigated thoroughly by Human Resources, Legal, and Security teams.
* Depending on severity, infractions may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, revocation of system access, and potential legal prosecution.
